# Disclaimer

**This template is not medical advice, and nothing produced with it is.**

It is a working method for people who want to analyze their own health data themselves,
shared as inspiration. The authors are not acting as clinicians, and an AI assistant
following these files is not one either.

## What that means in practice

1. **No diagnosis.** An analysis can say how a measurement compares with a published
   threshold, and how confident that comparison is. It cannot tell you that you have, or
   do not have, a condition.
2. **No treatment decisions.** Never start, stop or change prescribed medication or any
   treatment based on an analysis made with this template, and never pause a prescribed
   medication to test something. Those decisions belong with a clinician who knows your
   full history. Everyday habits (sleep, exercise, ordinary food) are your own business,
   but if you have a condition or take medication, talk to your clinician before changing
   them as well.
3. **Thresholds are borrowed, not invented.** Every threshold must come from a cited
   source. If no source exists, the analysis says so. A number without a source is not a
   threshold.
4. **Uncertainty is part of the answer.** Every conclusion states what it does *not*
   answer and how confident it is.
5. **Escalate rather than wait.** If an analysis meets one of the escalation triggers in
   [METHOD.md § 9](METHOD.md#9-escalation-triggers-written-before-the-result), or if you
   have new or worrying symptoms, contact a healthcare professional. If something feels
   urgent, contact emergency services. Do not wait for an analysis.

## Your data and your privacy

You are responsible for where your data is stored and who can see it. Keep your copy of
this repository private, keep raw data outside it ([DATA.md](DATA.md)), and think
carefully before analyzing data about anyone else. Other people's data needs their
consent.

**Whatever your AI assistant reads is sent to the company that runs it.** Keeping data out
of the repository does not keep it away from the AI. Before you point an assistant at your
data folder, read that provider's privacy and data retention terms, and leave out files
you would not share with them.
